About India

Introduction

India is one of the world’s oldest civilizations, known for its rich heritage, cultural diversity, languages, traditions, and breathtaking landscapes. Home to over 1.4 billion people and 28 states, India offers a vivid combination of ancient and modern worlds.

Highlights of India

Rich Culture

India is known for its festivals, classical dance forms, handicrafts, and traditions that change from state to state.

Diverse Cuisine

From spicy curries to sweets, each region has its own unique flavors and traditional dishes.

Stunning Landscapes

India has mountains, beaches, deserts, forests, and rivers — a paradise for nature lovers.

Major Characteristics of India (List)

  • World’s largest democracy
  • Home to multiple religions
  • 22 official languages
  • Fourth-largest economy (PPP)
  • Birthplace of Yoga, Ayurveda, and Buddhism

Historical Timeline (Ordered List)

  1. Indus Valley Civilization (3300–1300 BCE)
  2. Maurya & Gupta Empires — Golden Age
  3. Mughal Empire — Cultural Peak
  4. British Colonial Rule
  5. Independence (1947) & Modern India
